FFT ANALYSIS REPORT
===================

Dataset: 200 training images (100 real, 100 fake)
Image preprocessing: Grayscale, resized to 128x128
Method: 2D FFT followed by fftshift
Energy: Squared magnitude of the FFT
Frequency bands:
Low: radius <= 16
Mid: 16 < radius <= 32
High: radius > 32

MEAN AND STANDARD DEVIATION
---------------------------
Low frequency - Real: 0.988915 (SD: 0.005476), Fake: 0.989890 (SD: 0.005500)
Mid frequency - Real: 0.008219 (SD: 0.004126), Fake: 0.007480 (SD: 0.004021)
High frequency - Real: 0.002865 (SD: 0.001411), Fake: 0.002630 (SD: 0.001555)

OBSERVATION
-----------
The frequency energy distributions overlap between real and fake images.
These exploratory FFT features alone do not establish reliable classification.
Further evaluation is required before drawing conclusions.
